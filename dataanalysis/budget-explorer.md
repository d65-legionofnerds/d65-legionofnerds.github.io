# D65 Budget Explorer

An interactive tool for exploring how District 65 spends its Educational Fund budget. Select categories using the preset buttons or the checkbox tree to see spending as a share of the budget, in dollars, or per pupil over time.

Check one or more **peer districts** to compare D65 against the same comparable districts used in the [enrollment analysis](enrollment-data.html). In compare mode, the selected categories are summed into a single total and plotted as one line per district — letting you ask, for example, "how does D65's per-pupil special-education spending compare to its peers?"

**Data source:** ISBE Annual Financial Reports (FY2012–FY2025) for each district plus D65's adopted FY2026 budget, via districtvitals.com. On-behalf/pension payments are excluded to focus on district-controlled spending. Per-pupil figures use each district's official 9-month ADA (the ISBE Operating-Expense-Per-Pupil denominator). Because districts split costs across the Education, O&M, and Transportation funds differently, cross-district *levels* are approximate — comparing shares of budget and trends is more robust.
