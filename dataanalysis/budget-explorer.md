# D65 Budget Explorer

An interactive tool for exploring how District 65 spends its Educational Fund budget. Select categories using the preset buttons or the checkbox tree below the chart to see spending as a percentage of total budget (or in dollars) over time.

**Data source:** ISBE Annual Financial Reports, FY2012–FY2025, plus the adopted FY2026 budget. On-behalf/pension payments are excluded to focus on district-controlled spending.
