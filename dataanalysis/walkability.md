# Walkability Analysis

## Comparison of Walkability for Scenarios 1A, 1B, 2DR, and 2FR

This analysis uses data made available by District 65 on the [School Closure Hub](https://www.district65.net/about/budget-finance/structural-deficit-reduction-plan/phase-iii-school-closures-hub){:target="_blank"} to compare scenarios 1AR, 1BR, 2DR, and 2FR. Walkability is calculated by dividing the students with less than or equal to a .75 mile walk to school by the sum of all students. This uses IDOT hazards and hazards identified by D65.
