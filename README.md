# Who we are
The [Legion of Nerds](https://d65-legionofnerds.github.io/) is a group of concerned parents reviewing the Evanston D65 redistricting plan, validating its assumptions, and trying to identify if more solutions are available than closing schools outright. Combined, we have more than 200 years of professional experience working with data analysis, research methodology and evidence-informed decision-making across fields including public health, economics, engineering, finance, education and policy.

# Why we're doing this
We want to provide clarity into the methods of the process and how those affect the scenarios to the board. There are serious methodological concerns that we laid out to the administration.

These concerns are not about the community process or underlying goals. The concerns are about how the methodological choices of the process have predetermined the results.

# Updating the website
The site is built from this repo with Jekyll and published automatically when changes reach `main`.
To publish a new analysis you add **one Markdown file** with a few lines of settings at the top; the
homepage, section pages, All stories and search update themselves.

**Read [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) first.** It covers the steps, the settings for each page, the house
style (the yellow highlighter and the red margin notes), charts, and how to check your work. If you
use Claude Code or another AI assistant, it reads those instructions automatically.

A starting template for a new analysis is in [tools/templates/new-analysis.md](tools/templates/new-analysis.md).
