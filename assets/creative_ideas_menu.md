# Menu of Creative Ideas for City-D65 Collaboration

*Sourced from the community[^1]*

## City Coordination

- Share the cost of crossing guards with the City
- Share the cost of Foster School pedestrian crossings and lights
- Allow additional ticket revenue to benefit D65
- Shared maintenance programs for buildings and grounds
- Commodity purchasing
- Shared outsourced services
- Coordinated capital improvements
- Establish Special Service Areas
- Shared administrative services office space
- Shared administrative services (personnel)

## Facility Partnerships

- Food pantry
- Public library branch
- Field house

## Other Ideas

- Event space
- Theater groups
- Health centers
- Elderly services
- Early childhood education
- Tutoring center
- Community education services
- Arts classes
- Lease kitchen space for caterers
- Collaboration with education students in higher education
- Bolster special education programs so that students being sent to private schools could remain in the District

## Revenue Generation

- Cell tower leasing
- Solar panel leasing
- Corporate sponsorship on District facilities
